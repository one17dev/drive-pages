Privacy policy

rclone Fedora Drive is a personal configuration of the open-source rclone tool, used only by the owner of bergman.live on their own computer.

  • Data accessed: with the owner's consent, the app requests access (drive) to the owner's own Google Drive files and file metadata.
  • Use: files are listed, opened, uploaded, renamed, moved or deleted only when the owner does so on their own computer, so their Google Drive can be used like a local folder. Deleted files go to Google Drive's Trash. Files are never shared.
  • Storage: recently opened or saved files may be cached temporarily on the owner's computer. Authorization credentials are stored encrypted on that computer. No data is sent to the app developer or to any third party, and nothing is stored on any server.
  • Sharing and sale: no Google user data is shared, transferred or sold.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
